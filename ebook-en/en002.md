## 2.1 Core Definition: The Three Pillars

Archi is defined by three fundamental characteristics that make it unique in the Enterprise Architecture (EA) tool market:

1. **Open Source**: Full transparency and high flexibility for customization.

2. **Cross-Platform**: Runs seamlessly on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

3. **ArchiMate Centric**: Specifically designed to create and manage ArchiMate models, keeping pace with the latest standards from The Open Group.

## 2.2 Target Audience and Role-Based Modeling

Archi is not just for dedicated Enterprise Architects; its intuitive design supports all architectural roles:

- **Business Architects**: Modeling value streams, capabilities, and business processes.

- **Application/Solution Architects**: Mapping application components and data flows.

- **Technology/Infrastructure Architects**: Visualizing nodes, networks, and system deployments.

- **Integration/Information Architects**: Defining how data moves across the enterprise layers.

## 2.3 The Tool Landscape: Archi vs. Commercial Alternatives

The Open Group maintains a register of certified ArchiMate tools. While Archi is not positioned as a "commercial vendor" for certification purposes, it remains highly compatible.

- **Commercial Giants**: Tools like BizzDesign, Sparx Systems (Enterprise Architect), and Visual Paradigm offer extensive feature sets but come with high licensing costs.

- **Open Source Alternatives**: Tools like Modelio also offer modeling capabilities but often have a broader focus (UML, BPMN), whereas Archi is 100% focused on ArchiMate, providing a cleaner experience for EA modeling.

- **The "Venture" Advantage**: Many commercial tools introduce proprietary metadata. Archi stays "pure", ensuring your models are standards-compliant and portable.

## 2.4 The Archi Ecosystem and Community

One of Archi’s greatest strengths is its active development and support network:

- **Archi Forum**: An extremely active community where the lead developer frequently responds to bug reports and feature requests in real-time.

- **GitHub Project**: The source code and core plugins (like the co-modeling/Git plugin) are open for contribution.

- **ArchiMate 101**: It is highly recommended to study the ArchiMate 3.2 specification alongside Archi, as the tool is a direct implementation of the language's logic.
